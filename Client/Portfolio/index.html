How It Works

Simple, transparent, and no jargon

01

Discovery Call

A 20-minute chat about your business — what's working, what's not, and what you want your website to do. No jargon, no hard sell.

02

Design & Build

I design and build your site, checking in with you at key milestones. You'll see progress throughout — not just a big reveal at the end.

03

Review & Refine

Two rounds of revisions to make sure everything feels right. Copy, images, layout — we fine-tune until you're genuinely happy.

04

Launch & Handover

Your site goes live. Then we do a full training session so you know how to update everything yourself. You'll get a recorded walkthrough and a written guide.

I'll recommend the right platform for your business — whether that's a simple drag-and-drop builder or something fully custom-built.
