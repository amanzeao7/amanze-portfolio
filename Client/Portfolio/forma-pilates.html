Our Studio

Pilates that meets you where you are


FORMA was founded on a simple belief: Pilates should be accessible, challenging, and deeply personal. Whether you're recovering from injury, training for performance, or simply looking for a practice that fits your life — we're here for it.

Our Clapham studio is home to 12 Balanced Body reformers, a team of passionate instructors, and a community that lifts each other up. Classes are capped at 12 so you always get the attention you deserve.
