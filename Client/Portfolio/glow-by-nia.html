About Me

Hi, I'm Nia


I'm a certified lash technician based in Welling, South East London. I trained at an accredited academy and I've been perfecting my craft for over 3 years.

I work from my private home studio — it's clean, calm, and all about you. Every appointment is one-to-one, so you get my full attention and a set that's completely tailored to you.

I use only premium Korean silk lashes and medical-grade adhesive. Your natural lashes always come first.

Fully insured · Certified · Patch tests required
